The common aroma molecules, by volatility

A curated, open index of widely-used fragrance chemicals — grouped into top, heart and base notes by how fast they evaporate. Smaller/lighter molecules flash off first (top); the largest linger for hours (base). Naturals are represented by their signature molecule (e.g. black pepper → rotundone, vetiver → khusimol). Proposed as a public-good extension of GenesisL1's MOLNFT molecular corpus.
